# DMT Video Requirements — studied from the proven TikTok catalog

Source study (Jul 2026): "DMT: Ego Death Part 1" (1M+ views), "Part 2" (200K+),
"DMT: I Met An Alien", "DMT: The Waiting Room", "DMT: The Room Before Birth"
(current TikTok breakout; psychedelic fractal slides praised in comments).

## THE TITLING RULE (hard requirement)
**"DMT" appears in a title ONLY when the video is actually about DMT** — a narrator
recounting a DMT experience, or direct teaching about the DMT state. A passing
mention does not qualify. Consciousness/perception videos NEVER borrow the prefix.
Format when it applies: `DMT: <The Place or Event>` — e.g. "DMT: The Waiting Room".

## What made them special — the aura

### Two proven story shapes
**A. First-person testimony** (Room Before Birth, I Met An Alien, Waiting Room)
1. Confessional hook that disarms disbelief: "I swear... I know how that sounds, but listen."
2. Fast body-dissolution: "my body was gone — no hands, no breathing, not even a name."
3. A PLACE with concrete impossible geography (white space of soul-lights; a wall of
   moving lives; the waiting room between form and formlessness).
4. A PRESENCE — felt, not seen; "I felt like I had always known it."
5. A shown revelation, personal and specific: mother, childhood house, the people who
   hurt you, "what their pain would force me to discover inside myself."
6. A choice or question posed BY the presence ("Do you still want this life?") and the
   narrator's answer ("Yes — not because it would be easy. Because it would be mine.").
7. Collapse + return to the ordinary room. Real-footage grounding beat.
8. End on an open question handed to the viewer — never a tidy moral.

**B. Guided immersion teaching** (Ego Death 1 & 2 — the 1M formula)
1. Second-person plunge: "So you took a little puff... and somewhere between the
   inhale and the infinity, you vanished."
2. Name the phenomenon mythically: "Welcome to the Great Unmasking."
3. Alternate immersion and decode: "Let's break this down… Now let's get symbolic."
4. One ruling metaphor system (mask / curtain / spotlight / screen / filter).
5. Reframe the fear beat: "It SHOULD panic — it was never built to step outside the dream."
6. Source/the Field speaks directly: "You were never those things. You were me all along."
7. Symbol-decode section: mirrors, voids, funerals = the subconscious talking to itself.
8. Series cliffhanger: "what's been watching the whole time… see Part 2."

### Visual aura (both shapes)
- **Persistent glowing title pill** (purple glow) stays on screen the ENTIRE video.
- **One luminous central symbol per slide** on near-black: eclipse ring, white mask,
  theater curtain, exploding-light head, golden portal ring. Slide = one thought.
- **Psychedelic fractal/mandala slides** for the trance: kaleidoscope symmetry, torus
  tunnels, breathing geometry (Room Before Birth's breakout ingredient).
- Palette: deep violet/purple + electric blue + gold on black. High contrast.
- **One real-human grounding shot** near the return beat (couch, bed, child, window).
- Slide-based stills with slow zoom/drift beat stock footage here; ~6–10s per slide.
- 2:00–2:40 runtime; loop or part-2 hook at the end.

### Voice
Awed, sincere, tender at the core; wry humor allowed ("pipe down, lungs full",
"the universe flipped the channel"). Never clinical, never preachy. The presence's
lines land soft and certain.
